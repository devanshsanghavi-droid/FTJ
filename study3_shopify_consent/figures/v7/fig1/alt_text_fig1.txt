Three-panel figure. (a) Line drawing of a Shopify store in the browser, as a frame holding two shaded regions. In the page region, a cookie banner and vendor tags sit beside the consent tool's script blocker, a black wall that stops the tags. Below the page, three signals (the consent tool's choice, the region default and Global Privacy Control) enter Shopify's consent gate, which starts pixels in the sandbox region, a web worker and a sandboxed iframe; their requests, drawn in red, leave the browser for the ad vendors, listed by name in a box outside the frame, underneath the blocker, which covers the page only. Small circled numerals one to five mark the points where consent is lost, explained in the caption; for the headline, a vendor that the consent tool lists as blocked fired from the sandbox anyway on 46 of 89 stores. (b) Two-row event plot of one page load on the two shades of (a), timed from the banner becoming visible: the blocker stops TikTok's tag 2.7 seconds after navigation start, while TikTok's sandboxed pixel sends 5 library loads and 2 data beacons between 2.4 and 3.2 seconds, after the banner became visible at 1.3 seconds and with no choice made. (c) Raster of the 205 banner stores whose sandboxed pixels sent an ad request before any choice, one row each, sorted by the first request, with each request as a grey tick and the first requests joined by a red line, against time relative to the banner becoming visible (an amber line at zero); for 74% of them the first request came before the banner was visible, and the area between the red line and the amber line is shaded pale red.
