Two-panel figure. (a) Left: dot plot with 95% confidence intervals, one row per consent-management product with enough stores under both of Shopify's region defaults (Pandectes, OneTrust, Cookiebot, Osano), then a summary row for all banner stores. In every row the share of stores whose sandboxed ad pixel sent a request before any choice is higher under 'allowed until choice' (brick circles) than under 'denied until consent' (slate squares): over all banner stores 70% against 24%. Groups of fewer than 10 stores are grey, as a note in the plot says; a pale brick band spans the gap in each row whose groups are both coloured. Right, on the same rows and scale: the difference, 'allowed' minus 'denied', as a diamond with its interval against a grey zero line, +45.6 percentage points pooled within the 12 products (labelled 'Within product', level with the middle of the product rows, which a grey bracket groups) and +46.3 over all banner stores (labelled 'Crude', on their row), both intervals well above zero. (b) Paired dot plot of the same measure by the purpose the firing pixel declares, same markers and bands: 'marketing' 62% against 7%; not 'marketing' 26% against 19%; no purpose at all 19% against 11%.
