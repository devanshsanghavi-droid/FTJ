Two-panel figure. (a) Thin unit bars, one cell per store, with a dot and 95% confidence interval under each: among blocklist stores whose consent tool had not itself consented, the share on which a vendor that the blocklist declares blocked still sent a request from Shopify's sandbox before any choice is 50% in the main sample (7 of 14) and 52% in the expansion (39 of 75); in both bars the brick cells fill about half. Below, the combined estimate is 52% (46 of 89). (b) Left: dot plot with 95% confidence intervals, one pair of rows per Shopify region default; blocklist stores (brick circles) fired at least as often as stores without a readable declaration (grey squares): 90% vs 71% under 'allowed until choice' and 20% vs 17% under 'denied until consent', with the gap between each pair shaded pale brick. Right, on two rows of its own between the pairs: pooled over both defaults (diamonds with 95% confidence intervals, against a grey zero line), blocklist stores fired +13.4 pp (percentage points) more often in the combined sample, an interval entirely above zero, and +13.6 pp in the main sample alone, an interval that includes zero.
