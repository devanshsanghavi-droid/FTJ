## 1. What was measured

- **B1 expansion (Shopify, Tranco 200k–1M).** 120 candidate stores; 120 passed the robots.txt check and were loaded (0 without the test code because robots.txt disallowed the tagged address); 360 load records.
- **B2 beyond-Shopify.** 195 selected sites; 190 loaded; 570 load records.
- Method: `crawl/crawl.js` run unchanged through `round4/expansion/crawl/crawl_r4.js` (plan and output substitutions; for B2 also first-party URL/body retention, Set-Cookie names and architecture readouts). Arms A0 Default, A1 Decline only (then one reload), A2 GPC injected in every frame and worker. Fresh profile per load, default headless UA, robots.txt checked before the first load, at most 6 browsers, at least 10 s between loads of a site and 1 s between navigations.
